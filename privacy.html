Privacy Policy

Phut 91 Uploader stores only what it needs to post on the owner's behalf.

  • Access tokens granted at sign-in are stored on the owner's own computer and never shared.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share data about any other user or viewer.
  • The app reads only the basic profile of the signed-in owner, to confirm which account receives the upload.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time in the platform's account settings, which stops the app at once.
